PIN lock

Convenience lock only – not encryption. It keeps casual snoopers out when your phone is unlocked. Your data on this device is not encrypted. Only a salted hash of the PIN is stored. If you forget it, the only way back in is to erase all app data (offered on the lock screen).

Shift reminder

Remind me to log hours (off by default)

How it works, honestly: if you haven't logged a shift for today or yesterday, a banner shows when you open the app. A phone notification is best-effort only: this app has no server, so it can't wake your phone. On iPhone, web apps can show notifications only when added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+), and even then only while the app is open or recently active. Android is similar. Don't rely on it; the banner on open is the dependable part.

Peg boards Local 19 boards

Your own record of the physical peg boards (admins can also share a board live with the whole chat room; shared boards are read-only for everyone else). Pick a board, tap a peg in the legend (or a peg already on a slip) and then tap a slip circle to put it there. The app never decides who gets a job. To move a slip itself, press and hold it, then drag; or open the slip and use Move.

Today's needs

Ships coming

Letter photos stay on this device only (private, offline). Auto-removed 30 days after the ship's ETD.

Heads up: vessel schedules change often (delays, early arrivals, cancellations) and this list can lag by hours. It does not show labor needs. Shift/labor needs come from ILWU dispatch. "Track" opens a VesselFinder name search in a new tab (no login or app popups); open the vessel there for live position and delays (free positions can lag). Ships coming lists Seattle container terminals only (T5, T18); T91 is cruise, no container calls. The Needs At berth now box covers more ports. ⚙️ Gear opens a lashing/cone/catwalk card for that ship, filled in by coworkers and shared in your chat room (photos stay on your phone).

Sources: Seattle and Tacoma: Northwest Seaport Alliance Vessel Schedule Forecast (Tacoma includes car ships and breakbulk). Aberdeen: Port of Grays Harbor vessel schedule (dates only). Olympia: Port of Olympia vessel announcements (dates only). Everett: Port of Everett shipping schedule. Port Angeles: Port of Port Angeles ship schedule. Longview and Vancouver WA: no public schedule; Columbia River Pilots dispatch is used only when it is current. "leaves ?" means the source gives no departure time.
