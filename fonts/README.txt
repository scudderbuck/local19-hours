Alfa Slab One (regular, subset)
This folder has a small subset of Alfa Slab One containing only the
characters in "LONGSHORE LIFE", for the new-domain splash title.

Alfa Slab One is the face used on Longshore Life merch (badges and
shirts). Color treatment on the splash matches merch cream/tan
#eed6a0 (238, 214, 160).
