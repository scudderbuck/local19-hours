Anton (regular, subset)
This folder has a small subset of Anton containing the characters in
"LONGSHORE LIFE" and "WORK THE WATERFRONT", for the new-domain splash
title. Anton is the closest OFL match to the Waterfront shirt's heavy
condensed sans block lettering (compared with Bebas Neue, League Gothic,
Oswald Bold, and Big Shoulders Display Black).

Fill color is merch gold #ffda59 (sampled from the shirt letters) with a
distressed texture; the tagline is merch orange-red #ff3b00.
